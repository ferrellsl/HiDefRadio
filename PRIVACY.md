# OpenDial privacy policy

OpenDial does not collect, send or share any personal data. This applies to the Android app and the Windows version.

**What stays on your device.** Your favorites, station names, settings, station logos, album art you chose to block, saved weather radar frames and remembered tuner gains are kept on your phone, tablet or PC only. On Android, Settings > Clear saved data, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling removes them. If Android's own backup is switched on, Android may include them in the backup in your Google account. On Windows they are kept in your user folders (`%APPDATA%\HiDefRadio` and `%LOCALAPPDATA%\HiDefRadio`).

**No internet.** The radio, the station names, the album art and the traffic and weather maps all come over the air from the dongle. The app does not go online, except to connect to an rtl_tcp server that you set up yourself, and to open a web page when you tap a link such as this one.

**Recordings.** Recordings are saved only to the folder you choose, and are never sent anywhere.

**Permissions.** The app asks for USB access to talk to the RTL-SDR dongle, for notifications so it can keep playing in the background, and, only if you use an rtl_tcp server on your home network, for access to nearby devices.

No account, no ads, no tracking.
